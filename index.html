Monica Shanmugam

PhD candidate working on experimental fluid dynamics, CFD, and digital twins for propulsion.

I'm a PhD candidate in Aerospace Engineering at Virginia Tech. I combine physics-based simulation with wind-tunnel and engine experiments to build models that can be checked against real hardware.

I'm a graduate research assistant in the Stability Wind Tunnel and the Advanced Propulsion and Power Laboratory. On an NSF digital-twin project I lead the forward model for aero-engine health under particle damage, coupling high-fidelity CFD with a one-dimensional thermodynamic cycle. My master's thesis was the NASA BeVERLI Hill campaign: laser measurements of a three-dimensional turbulent boundary layer, used as a blind benchmark for turbulence models.
